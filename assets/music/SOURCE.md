# Music

All tracks were downloaded from OpenGameArt.org on 2026-11-17. The files are
unchanged; only the file names were changed. They loop in the game (src/music.cpp).

| `hall_throne_room.mp3` | Throne hall, after the key is taken | [Throne Room](https://opengameart.org/content/throne-room) (`Of Far Different Nature - Throne Room (CC-BY).mp3`) | Of Far Different Nature (https://fardifferent.carrd.co/) | [CC-BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| `garden_cathedral_in_the_forest.ogg` | Inner garden | [Cathedral in the forest (ambient loop)](https://opengameart.org/content/cathedral-in-the-forest-ambient-loop) (`forest_2.ogg`) | congusbongus | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
